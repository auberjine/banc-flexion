# Informations douanieres des pieces

Textes a copier dans le formulaire « Informations douanieres » de l'usineur, en
anglais comme il le demande. Une section par fichier STEP, dans l'ordre
alphabetique : le formulaire affiche le nom du fichier (« Nom : tourillon.step »),
c'est la cle. Le repere et la designation francaise ne servent qu'au controle.

Toutes les pieces fabriquees du banc figurent ici ; il n'y en a que 13 dans la
commande, les autres se sautent.

## Code SH (facultatif)

**9024.90** pour toutes les pieces : *parts and accessories of machines and
appliances for testing the mechanical properties of materials*. Le banc est un
appareil d'essai des materiaux et ces pieces ne servent qu'a lui.

Si le transitaire prefere un classement par matiere : 7326.90 (*other articles
of iron or steel*) pour l'acier, 7419.80 (*other articles of copper*) pour les
deux plaques en bronze. Le champ etant facultatif, on peut aussi le laisser vide.

## Texte unique (bouton « Copier les informations pour toutes les pieces »)

- **Name** : Part of a laboratory bending test rig
- **Function** : Custom-made metal part of a laboratory three-point bending test rig used to test concrete beams (materials testing apparatus, research use).

## Texte par piece

### coin.step -- 02c Coin de commande
- **Name** : Drive wedge
- **Function** : Threaded steel wedge (12 degrees) moved by a screw to apply the load in a laboratory three-point bending test rig for concrete beams.

### coulisseau.step -- 02a Coulisseau a tete inclinee
- **Name** : Loading slide block
- **Function** : Guided steel block of the loading head of a laboratory three-point bending test rig for concrete beams; pushed down by the wedge, it compresses the disc springs that apply the load.

### crochet.step -- 05c Crochet d'etuve
- **Name** : Oven hanging bracket
- **Function** : Steel bracket hooked into the perforated wall of a laboratory oven to hold a three-point bending test rig upright during tests at up to 150 degrees C.

### entretoise.step -- 06b Entretoise tubulaire
- **Name** : Frame spacer tube
- **Function** : Steel tube spacer clamped by a bolt between the two side plates of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; sets the spacing between the plates.

### entretoise_vis.step -- 07b Entretoise de butee
- **Name** : Thrust spacer tube
- **Function** : Steel tube spacer between the frame and the screw thrust plates of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; carries the screw reaction force in compression.

### flanc.step -- 01 Flanc en treillis
- **Name** : Frame side plate
- **Function** : Steel side plate (truss) of the load frame of a laboratory three-point bending test rig for concrete beams; carries the test load between the beam supports and the loading head.

### patin_appui.step -- 04a Patin d'appui rainure
- **Name** : Grooved support pad
- **Function** : Steel pad bonded under the concrete test beam at a support of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; its groove sits on the support edge of the frame.

### patin_charge.step -- 04b Patin de charge
- **Name** : Crowned loading pad
- **Function** : Steel pad bonded on top of the concrete test beam at mid-span in a laboratory three-point bending test rig; its crowned top face receives the load from the loading head.

### pied.step -- 05 Pied a mi-bois
- **Name** : Frame foot plate
- **Function** : Steel foot plate slotted onto the side plates of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; supports the rig on a bench or in an oven (rig weight only).

### plat_renfort.step -- 04c Plat de renfort colle
- **Name** : Steel reinforcing strip
- **Function** : Flat steel strip bonded under the concrete test beam of a laboratory three-point bending test rig to reinforce the specimen (one pair per test).

### plaquette_basse.step -- 06d Plaque de frottement, dessous
- **Name** : Bronze slide plate (lower)
- **Function** : Machined bronze wear plate screwed under the drive wedge of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; sliding surface between the wedge and the inclined slide block.

### plaquette_haute.step -- 06c Plaque de frottement, dessus
- **Name** : Bronze slide plate (upper)
- **Function** : Machined bronze wear plate screwed on top of the drive wedge of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; sliding surface between the wedge and the crossbeam.

### poussoir.step -- 02b Plateau de poussoir
- **Name** : Pusher plate
- **Function** : Steel plate of the bolted pusher block of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; transmits the load from the disc spring stack to the loading pad on the beam.

### support.step -- 07 Platine de butee de la vis
- **Name** : Screw thrust plate
- **Function** : Steel plate holding the loading screw axially on the frame of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; takes the screw reaction force.

### tourillon.step -- 06a Tourillon de centrage
- **Name** : Centering pin
- **Function** : Cylindrical steel pin bonded into the slide block of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; centres the disc spring stack and guides the pusher block.

### traverse.step -- 03 Plaque de traverse
- **Name** : Crossbeam plate
- **Function** : Steel plate of the crossbeam (six plates bolted together) of a laboratory three-point bending test rig; the drive wedge slides under it and it transfers the load to the side plates.
